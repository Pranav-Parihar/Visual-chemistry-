Lab Safety — Secondary & Senior Secondary

  • Wear cotton lab coat + goggles. Never taste chemicals; waft gases.
  • Never point heating test-tube mouth at yourself or others.
  • Titration: rinse burette with titrant, remove air bubbles, read lower meniscus (clear) / upper meniscus (KMnO4).
  • Qualitative analysis: use 10–20 mg salt, prepare clear Original Solution, pass H2S slowly.
  • Handle conc. acids, CS2, Na metal, conc. HNO3 only under teacher supervision.
